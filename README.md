## About me

I work across the areas where financial clarity, operational reality, and
strategic decisions need to meet. My experience spans finance leadership,
controllership, FP&A, international operations, business planning, and
financial governance.

I am interested in the decisions behind the visible result—whether those
decisions concern a business, a wardrobe, a home, or a life built for the long
term.

## HFF

I created **HFF**, an independent editorial project exploring intentional
shopping, personal style, quality, pricing, and the psychology behind a good
find.

It is not about buying more. It is about understanding what deserves a place in
your wardrobe, your life, and your budget.
